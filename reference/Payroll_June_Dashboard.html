How each person's pay is calculated

Every worker has a daily time card. The book records the clock-in and clock-out time each day, then:

  1. Hours worked = clock-out − clock-in (times written as hours.minutes, e.g. 11.10 = 11:10).
  2. Each full day counts 7h30m as the basic day and 1 hour lunch is removed. Everything above that is overtime.
  3. At month end: overtime minutes are added up properly, then 9 minutes per working day is deducted from total overtime (the "R2" adjustment in the book).
  4. Saturday overtime and public holiday work are kept separate and paid at a higher rate. June 1 was the public holiday.
  5. Everyone gets a fixed RM 200 allowance, and the mid-month advance (usually RM 550) is subtracted at the end.
